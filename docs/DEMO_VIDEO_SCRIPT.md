# VOTR Demo Video Script

## Option A: Record the HTML Presentation

1. Open `docs/demo/demo.html` in a browser (Chrome/Safari)
2. Use QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) or OBS to record
3. Click **Next** through the 6 slides (or use arrow keys)
4. Suggested timing: 8–10 seconds per slide (~1 min total)
5. Optional: Add voiceover using the script below

---

## Option B: Record the Live App

1. **Start backend**: `cd backend && npm start`
2. **Start mobile**: `cd mobile && npx expo start` → press `i` for iOS
3. Record the simulator while you:

   | Step | Action | Duration |
   |------|--------|----------|
   | 1 | App opens → Splash → Address entry | 10s |
   | 2 | Enter address (e.g. 525 N Tryon St, Charlotte, NC 28202) | 15s |
   | 3 | Complete onboarding—swipe 3–4 issues right | 15s |
   | 4 | Feed loads → tap a candidate | 10s |
   | 5 | Candidate detail → Add to Roster | 5s |
   | 6 | Tab to My Roster → show saved candidates | 8s |
   | 7 | Tab to Journey → show XP, badges | 5s |
   | 8 | Tab to Profile → show address, elections link | 5s |

   Total: ~1:15

---

## Voiceover Script (for HTML or Live)

**Slide 1 / Splash**  
> VOTR makes democracy sexy. Discover candidates that match your values.

**Slide 2 / Address**  
> Enter where you’re registered to vote. We use your address to show your real ballot.

**Slide 3 / Onboarding**  
> Swipe on the issues that matter to you—climate, healthcare, education. We use this to score how well candidates align with your views.

**Slide 4 / Feed**  
> Here’s who’s running. Candidates are grouped by office, with match scores based on your preferences.

**Slide 5 / Roster**  
> Add candidates you support. Your roster syncs across devices when you sign in.

**Slide 6 / Journey**  
> Earn XP, keep streaks, and unlock badges. Take the policy quiz to level up your civic knowledge.

**Closing**  
> VOTR. Your vote. Your voice.
